Build Nests for Europe’s Rarest Seal

Lake Saimaa · 3 days · Winter

The project

Why this place matters

Lake Saimaa is home to the endangered Saimaa ringed seal. This experience shows how a traveller can learn about changing winter conditions, breeding habitat and conservation work before contributing alongside a project team.

Scientific partner

Who leads the work?

The real version would be built around an identified research team. This prototype keeps the partner generic until a collaboration is confirmed.

RT
Project partner Ecological restoration / conservation research Partner institution to be confirmed
What you will do

Hands-on, not sightseeing.

Learn how breeding habitat is monitored.
Prepare for winter field conditions.
Participate in a defined conservation activity.
Record observations under project guidance.
What you will learn

A field course, not a tour.

Ecology · conservation challenge · restoration logic · field observation · responsible participation.

Impact

What gets verified?

Your participation is a separate record from ecological impact. The future partner project would define which ecological indicators can legitimately be measured, calculated or projected.
