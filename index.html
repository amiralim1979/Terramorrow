Transparency

How we measure impact — and why it matters.

We don't estimate. We don't approximate for marketing. Every impact metric on TerraMorrow comes from the partner research institution's own scientific methodology — the same methods they use in their peer-reviewed work.

📐 What "estimated 10-year CO₂ sequestration" means

When we say a restored forest project sequesters an estimated 142 kg CO₂e over 10 years, that figure comes from standard forestry carbon sequestration models applied by the research team — not a marketing calculation. It is a projection, not a current measurement, and we label it as such. Read our impact methodology →
