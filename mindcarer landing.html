The Problem

Caregiving is invisible work — and it takes a real toll

General mental health apps were not built for you. They don't understand anticipatory grief, role reversal, caregiver guilt, or what it means to watch a parent slowly disappear.

🌑

Burnout creeps in silently

Caregiver burnout doesn't announce itself. It accumulates slowly — until one day you have nothing left to give.

🏚

You feel completely alone

1 in 4 caregivers report loneliness and social withdrawal. Few people around you truly understand what this is like.

💊

Guilt is constant

The ambivalence of loving someone and resenting the situation is real — and almost no one talks about it.

📋

No tool was built for this

Headspace and Calm weren't designed for the emotional reality of caring for an aging parent. MindCarer is.

How MindCarer Works

Clinically designed. Built around your reality.

Every feature was designed by a clinical psychologist who understands caregiver burnout from the inside out — not from a UX workshop.

01

Daily 90-second check-in

Five caregiver-specific questions that track your emotional patterns over time — not generic mood sliders.

02

Burnout risk score

Our clinically-validated algorithm detects burnout risk patterns 4–6 weeks before crisis — so you can act before you break.

03

CBT exercises for caregivers

3-minute evidence-based exercises for guilt, anticipatory grief, role reversal — the feelings you can't explain to others.

04

Peer community

Private groups with people who actually understand — organised by care situation: dementia, Parkinson's, end-of-life.
